The ECOWAS Trade Liberalisation Scheme (ETLS) is a regional trade mechanism designed to facilitate trade among ECOWAS Member States by providing preferential customs-duty treatment for eligible products originating within the Community.

20. Conclusion
ETLS is an important instrument for facilitating preferential trade in eligible originating goods across the ECOWAS region. Its effectiveness depends on clear origin rules, transparent approval procedures, reliable documentation, consistent customs implementation, and accessible information for businesses.
For practical decisions, always verify current requirements against the applicable ECOWAS legal instruments and the competent national authorities.
